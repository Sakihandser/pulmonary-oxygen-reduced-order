# Empirical comparison protocol

Date: 2026-10-30. This is a retrospective exploratory reanalysis, not a prospectively registered study.

1. Compare the three existing effective exchange laws (saturation relaxation, content relaxation, constant conductance), a pressure relaxation control, and an equilibrium plus effective venous admixture comparator. Each receives exactly one baseline parameter. Admixture is an effective whole-lung descriptor, not an identified anatomical shunt.
2. Use PaO2 in mmHg as the primary common observed endpoint. All candidates use identical measured PvO2, flow and Hb, and the common Hill curve. Saturation and content derived from this curve are model outputs, not additional independent measurements. Report mismatch of the common blood curve to available blood-gas saturations.
3. For Carta, Lichtblau and both Blanco studies, fit to the room-air resting baseline only; freeze the coefficient for the remaining conditions. Lichtblau reports cardiac index: use indexed capacity, with no assumed BSA. Studies with missing Hb are external-domain sensitivity tests, not primary PAH evidence. Do not infer Hb from the arterial outcome.
4. For the seven individual Dantzker observations, leave one patient out, fit the scalar on the remaining six by unweighted PaO2 least squares, and predict the held-out patient. This is internal cross-validation, not an independent external cohort. Additionally freeze the Carta air coefficient and transfer to all seven patients without refitting.
5. Reconstruct PA using the full alveolar gas equation: FiO2(PB-PH2O)-PaCO2[FiO2+(1-FiO2)/R]. Use the reported 717.7 mmHg Zurich pressure; R=0.8 is assumed, varied in sensitivity. When an A-a gradient is tabulated, Pa+A-a reconstructs the authors' calculated PA. It is not a separately measured alveolar pressure; shared rounding and blood-curve uncertainty remain.
6. Report errors by study and challenge; do not use cohort N as if aggregate means were independent participants, and do not pool hyperoxia with air breathing to select a universal winner. Training residuals are excluded from test metrics. Do not use hyperoxia-derived shunt to predict the same hyperoxia endpoint.
7. Sensitivity varies shared P50 (24, 26.8, 30 mmHg), Hill exponent (2.5, 2.7, 3), R (0.75, 0.8, 0.9), and missing Hb (12, 15, 18 g/dL). Baseline-output rounding is assessed separately. These are deterministic assumptions, not probability distributions or confidence intervals. No patient-level covariance or repeated-measures uncertainty is available.
8. Couple a conditionally favored model, if supported, to the unchanged vascular solver. Retain competing-law sensitivity if rankings are not stable. Network predictions are hypothetical steady-state mechanisms; no model establishes causal treatment benefit, treatment choice, or clinical oxygen thresholds.
9. Preserve authors, affiliations, contributions, funding, conflicts, ethics/data/AI declarations. Preserve historical files and their provenance. Deliver all transcriptions, calculations, figures, and limitations.

10. Primary-model refinement on 2026-10-30: adopt equilibrium with effective admixture for the PH mechanism study. Split the existing frozen external predictions into the source-table diagnoses D01–D04 (idiopathic PH) and D05–D07 (recurrent emboli), without refitting. This is a post-selection descriptive audit, not a new validation set. Report the small nominal saturation-model advantage in the four idiopathic cases.
11. Contrast the inherited Q(PVR) relation with Q clamped at the PVR=5 WU reference. At each PVR compute mPAP=PAWP+PVR*Q consistently. The clamped-flow counterfactual changes the cardiac closure and is not an alternative fit to patient outcomes. Then change one input at a time from the reference: Q down 25%, Hb to 10 g/dL, PA to 150 mmHg, s_eff to 0.30, or demand to 500 mL/min. These are chosen perturbations, not matched physiological effect sizes, intervention doses or treatment rankings.
12. Use the exact primary balance in both the network and counterfactual exporter. Return blank oxygen outputs with feasible=False for negative-Cv states. The Q_min boundary is purely algebraic. Keep the original numerical root as an independent test rather than silently imposing a minimum oxygen saturation. Configuration and source observations are covered by input_sha256.json; all release files are covered by the root manifest.
