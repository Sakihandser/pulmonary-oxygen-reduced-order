# Targeted literature selection and extraction

Search and source verification date: 2026-10-30. This is a targeted exploratory search and citation-following exercise, not a registered systematic review. No exhaustive screened-record count or PRISMA claim is made.

The search covered pulmonary hypertension with arterial and mixed-venous blood gases, hypoxia/hyperoxia during right-heart catheterization, exercise and multiple inert gas elimination measurements, sildenafil gas exchange in COPD-PH, inhaled nitric oxide in pulmonary fibrosis, and alveolo-capillary equilibration at altitude. Primary publisher pages, PubMed/Europe PMC full-text XML, the original JCI PDF and an author-hosted University of Barcelona thesis containing the primary papers were checked. Titles, DOI and table locations are listed in `data/source_registry.csv` so the search can be continued.

Eligibility for numerical scoring requires measured arterial PO2 and mixed-venous PO2, measured output or cardiac index, and a reported or reconstructable alveolar boundary. Reported Hb is preferred. Missing Hb is explicitly assumed only in the external COPD/IPF stress tests and varied in sensitivity. Saturation-only and calculated equilibration-only studies are contextual, not scored. Existing animal transfer and model-generated outputs are not added to the human accuracy ranking.

1. Carta: Table 2 means; kPa multiplied by 7.500616827. Barometric pressure 717.7 mmHg and water-vapor pressure 47.1 mmHg follow the methods. The shunt computed during hyperoxia is not used as an independent predictor of that endpoint. Available-case condition means are not assumed to be complete paired records for all 149 participants.
2. Lichtblau: Tables 2–3 means; cardiac index retained as L/min/m2. No BSA or total Q is invented. Zurich barometric boundary is shared with Carta and R is varied. Possible participant overlap between these reports is unresolved.
3. Dantzker: Table II, printed page 1052, seven individual columns. Original labels replaced by D01–D07 in the same order. PA is reconstructed as reported Pa plus reported A-a difference. This algebraically recovers the source gas-equation estimate, not an independent alveolar observation. Low-V/Q blood flow already includes shunt. Raw regional distributions are unavailable.
4. Blanco COPD: Tables 2–3, printed pages 272–273. Each post-sildenafil mean equals its baseline plus the tabulated mean change; e.g. resting Q=4.9+0.13 and Pa=64-6. Hb is not inferred from the target. Source paper accessible within the author's thesis at PDF pages 72–73 (one-based).
5. Blanco IPF: Tables 3–4 in the primary typeset paper included in the author's thesis, PDF pages 92 and 94 (one-based). Table 3 Q and pressures and Table 4 blood gases were checked visually. MIGET-predicted-minus-measured Pa is a derived discrepancy, not the alveolar input. Not all seven IPF patients have resting PH.
6. Stubbs: Table 1, two rest/exercise conditions, retained as context because arterial PO2/Hb/alveolar inputs are incomplete. Pulse SpO2 is not converted to an observed PaO2.
7. Beretta: Table 1 and methods, four sea-level/altitude rest/work conditions, retained as context because mixed-venous measurements are absent and some cardiac-output values are assumptions. Its calculated diffusion/equilibration output is not used as observed ground truth.

`make_data.py` is the cell-level extraction recipe; `observations.csv` retains study, condition, source table, DOI, input-unit basis, PA source, Hb status and caveats in every row. Selected numerical facts are attributed to their original publications. Source PDFs/XML remain in the local evidence archive and are excluded from distributable code and figure ZIPs.

Remaining evidence gaps include raw paired modern PAH data, individual pH/temperature-dependent blood curves, measured regional ventilation/perfusion, measured diffusion capacity across exercise, independent cardiac adaptation, and a further external cohort after model selection. These gaps limit claims even when a candidate has the lowest error among those tested.
