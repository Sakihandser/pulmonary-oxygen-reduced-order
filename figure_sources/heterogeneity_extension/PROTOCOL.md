# Baseline constrained exchange heterogeneity

Frozen before calculation on 14 November 2026. This exploratory extension follows the earlier model comparisons; it introduces no observations or fitted clinical parameters.

1. Test whether the single exponential derivative identifies arterial response when exchange paths are unresolved. Use fixed positive flow weights w_i, common end-capillary content Ce and systemic consumption M, Q_i=w_i Q, and path capacities K_i(Q)=K_i(Q0)(Q/Q0)^(1−beta). Set x_i=K_i(Q0)/(w_i Q0), z_i=exp(−x_i). Then s(r)=sum w_i z_i^(r^(−beta)). Every construction matches s(1)=0.20.
2. Derive sharp bounds over distributions of z in [L,U] with known mean s*. For r≥1 and 0≤beta≤1, concavity gives a chord lower bound and a homogeneous upper bound for s(r). Convert these into content bounds, intersect with Cv≥0, and classify arterial recovery at a specified flow as guaranteed, impossible, or unresolved. These are model-class bounds, not confidence intervals. Evaluate beta=0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1 at r=1,1.25,1.5,2, using [0,1], [0.001,0.8] and [0.05,0.5]. The latter two are illustrative support restrictions, not physiological estimates.
3. Construct a finite two-path counterexample with z=(0.001,0.8), weights fixed by s*=0.20. Match a second two-path model to both its mean exposure E[x] and its baseline s*: set the larger exposure to 20 and solve the smaller exposure and weight. Compare derivatives and full responses without changing either model's total capacity as Q varies (beta=1). Include homogeneous and ideal shunt/equilibrated limits as bounds, clearly separating their different total capacities.
4. Retain Q0=5 L/min, M=250 mL/min, Hb=14 g/dL and PA=100 mmHg. The original target uses s0=0.15. Report first upward arterial recovery only within r in [1,2], never extrapolate a failed bounded search to no recovery at any flow. Display r=1 to 2 in 101 steps. Also compare delivery and verify feasibility.
5. Derive the local moment h=E[−z ln z] and its chord/Jensen bounds. Test the sign ambiguity under matched mean exposure. Establish which delivery monotonicity result survives the heterogeneous extension at fixed weights, Ce and M. Examine deterministic random mixtures as checks, not statistical evidence.
6. Verify independently: solve mixed-venous balance numerically, finite-difference derivatives, recover homogeneous limits, check sharp-bound attaining distributions and finite approximations, match both counterexample moments, verify recovery classifications including infeasible classes, and test zero demand/invalid domains. Preserve all earlier observations and results. Full offline reproduction must include this extension; final figures must redraw from the supplied archive.

Known prior art includes flow-diffusion laws, Jensen's inequality, pulmonary heterogeneity modelling (Roy and Secomb 2019), and oxygen-extraction heterogeneity (Jespersen and Østergaard 2012). The extension will not claim these foundations or heterogeneity itself as new. Its intended contribution is an auditable baseline-constrained robustness analysis of the earlier compensation conclusion.
