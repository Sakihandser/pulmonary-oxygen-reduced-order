# Additional-data model selection and mechanism analysis

Protocol fixed before the first numerical score on 2026-11-10. This is retrospective exploratory reanalysis, not a registered prospective validation.

Primary task: predict arterial oxygen tension from a baseline calibration and current mixed venous tension, alveolar inputs and flow. Five unchanged single-parameter laws are saturation relaxation, oxygen-content relaxation, fixed conductance, pressure relaxation and effective admixture. Two zero-new-parameter comparators hold baseline Pa or baseline A-a difference constant. The primary score is equal-source mean squared Pa error, reported as its square root. MAE, signed bias, per-source scores and each prediction remain available. A study is not a patient; repeated source cohort sizes are never added.

Haefliger2025 adds four adjusted rest condition estimates with measured Pv, including two sildenafil states. Melot1983 adds seven duplicate-mean patient states (two people, three catheterization baselines); Hb is unreported and assigned 15 g/dL with 12–18 sensitivity. M01 has atrial shunting and is a transportability stress test, not typical modern PAH. Lichtblau2025 provides four rest/exercise means with Sv but no Pv: inverse-Sv inputs are secondary only. These two modern reports share the BASEC2020-02163 trial family and are grouped together irrespective of different NCT labels, posture and subset. The earlier Zurich reports are conservatively one cohort family.

All new alveolar pressures use FiO2, assumed local standard PB (717.7 Zurich; 760 Melot), water pressure 47.1 and PaCO2 with declared R. Test Pa and A-a are never used in this primary path. Existing source-reconstructed PA is replaced by the same gas equation for the new comparison; old results are preserved separately. Sensitivity varies P50 24/26.8/30, Hill 2.5/2.7/3, R 0.75/0.8/0.9/1.0, and missing Hb 12/15/18. All coefficients are recalibrated from the permitted baseline for every scenario.

Exploratory model: s(q)=exp(log(s0)*(q0/q)^beta), with beta in [-1,2]. beta=0 is fixed admixture; beta=1 reproduces content relaxation. Each unit's s0 is fixed from its baseline. Estimate beta only on non-test cohort families using equal-family errors, and predict the omitted family's conditions. This estimates a shared flow exponent, not a new anatomical shunt law. Report the complete profile and fold coefficients; do not claim superiority from all-data fit. Calibration baselines may be supplied for the held-out unit because the stated task is response prediction after baseline measurement. They are not part of the score.

Primary PH-family ranking excludes COPD/IPF and the derived-Pv exercise extension. External disease and secondary exercise scores are separate. Hyperoxia is reported separately because its mmHg scale can dominate a pooled score. Finite-domain violations are retained as failures, not silently dropped. No patient-level CI is derived from study means.

Mechanism work: derive exact thresholds for arterial content preservation and oxygen-delivery preservation, with independent changes in flow, demand, end-capillary content and effective admixture. Compare these distinct thresholds and identify discordant response regions. Retrospective state-specific s is a diagnostic reconstructed from outcomes, never inserted into a claimed prediction. Where source Q is Fick-derived, blood oxygen and Q do not independently validate mass balance. Model decompositions are algebraic explanations under assumptions, not identified causal treatment effects.
