# Data and software provenance

Project software is supplied under the root MIT LICENSE. The adapted external pressure-kernel comparison is supplied under the GPL-3.0 terms in sensitivity/LICENSE. Upstream attribution, source hash, pinned commit and license are supplied in sensitivity/external_alvin.

Published aggregate source cells are factual extractions attributed by study, group, stage, variable, unit and SD/SEM label. Source snapshot hashes document the source used for transcription; the publisher full text, table layouts and images are not bundled or sublicensed. The empirical extension includes seven recoded individual rows already published in Dantzker and Bower (1979), DOI 10.1172/JCI109542; original initials are not redistributed. No unpublished participant-level records are included. Other new observations are attributed group-condition means, with source DOI and table in every row. Article figures are generated from the supplied model results. The source authors have not endorsed or physiologically validated these models.


Additional selected numerical observations are attributed to Häfliger 2025 (10.3390/jcm14238407), Lichtblau 2025 (10.1164/rccm.202409-1737RL) and Mélot 1983 (10.1378/chest.83.2.203). Source articles and patient records are not redistributed. See selection_extension/data/additional_sources.csv for exact extraction locations and cohort dependencies.
